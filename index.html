MY FIRST DAY IN UNIZIK

My first day in UNIZIK was a horror.
It was so stressful that i had to board on a tricycle three times just to find the MICTU.
But i found the MICTU eventually but, i had to wait in line for me to be attended to.
It was not nice at all but i finally did my biometrics
Then i went to the Admin block for my clearance.

"Freshers, know what you are for!"

Things I learned on day 1:

  • Always ask for directions
  • Go early for clearance
  • Never trust google maps

My mistakes in order:

  1. Entered wrong tricycle
  2. Went to wrong block
  3. Joined wrong queue
